02 / About

Designing with clarity, care and context.

I enjoy untangling complex requirements and bringing people, processes and interfaces into alignment.

I make the complicated feel considered.

Most of my projects start with a long requirements document and a lot of “what happens if…” questions. I want to know how the whole system behaves before I draw a screen, because that's usually where the hard problems are.

I stay involved after handoff too: reviewing builds, chasing edge cases, and adjusting when real data doesn't look like the mockups.

My 5D process, built on the Double Diamond
  1. Discover through ethnographic research: watching people where the work happens
  2. Define the real problem, the roles and every state they can end up in
  3. Design the flows first, then prototype and test the screens
  4. Develop alongside engineers, reviewing every build
  5. Deliver it, then check it works the way people actually use it

End to end

I take work from requirements and user flows through to prototypes, final UI and implementation support.

Systems thinker

Role-based platforms, long workflows, dashboards, and the reusable patterns that hold them together.

Collaborative

I work closely with stakeholders, product managers and engineers, and I'd rather ask early than guess.

Always curious

Currently exploring how AI tools can speed up the boring parts of design work, so there's more time for the hard parts.
